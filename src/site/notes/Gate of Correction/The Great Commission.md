What exactly were we told to do by Jesus with his last words before he ascended?

The Literal translations is:

“Having gone therefore, disciple all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I commanded you; and behold, I am with you all the days, until the end of the age.”

But a more accurate translation is:

[[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Human Beings/Disciple\|Disciple]] the people, [[Gate of Correction/Matthew 28 v19-20 Into All the World\|Matthew 28 v19-20 Into All the World]]
- as you are going [[Gate of Correction/Matthew 28 v19-20 Go and Teach\|Matthew 28 v19-20 Go and Teach]]
- immerse them in the character, reputation, authority, and purpose of the Father, the Son, and the Set Apart Breath, [[Gate of Correction/Matthew 28 v19-20 Baptizing in the Name\|Matthew 28 v19-20 Baptizing in the Name]]
- teach them to keep, reserve, watch, preserve, hold fast, to attend to carefully, take care of, to guard [[Gate of Correction/Matthew 28 v19-20 Teaching to …\|Matthew 28 v19-20 Teaching to …]]
	- all Jesus has commanded
Remember
- I am with you (Emmanuel - literally god is with you)
	- always
	- to the end of the age
