# The Disciples' Initial Actions and the General Story of Jonah

| Element                                     | The Disciples                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Jonah                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Initial call                                | Called directly by Yeshua — "follow me, and I will make you fishers of men" (Matthew 4:19)                                                                                                                                                                            | Called directly by YHWH — "the word of Yahweh came to Jonah" (Jonah 1:1)                                 |
| Immediate response                          | "They immediately left their nets and followed him" (Matthew 4:20)                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Immediately flees in the opposite direction (Jonah 1:3)                                                  |
| Storm at sea                                | "A big wind storm arose... the boat was already filled" while Yeshua slept (Mark 4:37-38)                                                                                                                                                                             | "Yahweh sent out a great wind... the ship was likely to break up" while Jonah slept (Jonah 1:4-5)        |
| Waking the sleeper                          | "They woke him up, and told him, 'Teacher, don't you care that we are dying?'" (Mark 4:38)                                                                                                                                                                            | The captain wakes Jonah: "what do you mean, sleeper? Arise, call on your god!" (Jonah 1:6)               |
| Fear during the storm                       | "They were greatly afraid" (Mark 4:41)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | "Then the mariners were afraid" (Jonah 1:5)                                                              |
| Response once the danger passes             | "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?" (Mark 4:41)                                                                                                                                                                                              | "The men feared Yahweh exceedingly... offered a sacrifice... and made vows" (Jonah 1:16)                 |
| Commission to a wider audience              | "Go and make disciples of all nations" (Matthew 28:19)                                                                                                                                                                                                                | "Arise, go to Nineveh... preach to it the message that I give you" (Jonah 3:2)                           |
| Initial hesitation/doubt at that commission | Some "worshiped him; but some doubted" (Matthew 28:17)                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Jonah flees rather than go at all (Jonah 1:3)                                                            |
| Failure of nerve under pressure             | Peter denies Yeshua three times: "I don't know the man" (Matthew 26:69-74); the disciples "all left him, and fled" at His arrest (Mark 14:50)                                                                                                                         | Jonah flees his commission entirely before it begins (Jonah 1:3)                                         |
| Confronted afterward by the one they failed | Yeshua restores Peter directly: "Simon, son of Jonah, do you love me?" asked three times (John 21:15-17)<br><br>Jesus replied, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah, for this was not revealed to you by flesh and blood, but by my Father in heaven. (Matthew 16:17) | YHWH confronts Jonah directly with repeated questions: "is it right for you to be angry?" (Jonah 4:4, 9) |
| Resolution shown in the text                | Peter's restoration is completed and recorded (John 21:15-19; Acts 2:14)                                                                                                                                                                                              | Jonah's response to YHWH's final question is never recorded — the book ends unresolved (Jonah 4:11)      |

Peter is directly addressed as "Simon, son of Jonah" in Every major, ancient Greek manuscript of Matthew but not in John.
