# Define

- Greek: pneumatika (πνευματικά) — neuter plural of pneumatikos, "things of the Spirit" (πνεῦμα, pneuma), used at 1 Corinthians 14:1, "earnestly desire spiritual gifts" (WEB). The related term charisma (χάρισμα), "gift of grace/favor," names the same enablements throughout 1 Corinthians 12:4, 9, 28, 30-31.
- Hebrew: ruach (רוּחַ) — spirit/breath/wind, the operative force behind Spirit-given ability and service in the Tanakh (Exodus 31:3, Numbers 11:25, Judges 3:10, 1 Samuel 16:13). At the time of writing, "spiritual gifts" would be understood through this Hebrew framework — ruach coming upon a person to equip them for a task within the covenant community, not as an individual possession but as service to the whole.

# Related Words

- prophecy — propheteia (προφητεία), 1 Corinthians 14:1, 3, 5, 22, 24, 39
- [[Gate of Wisdom/T/tongues\|tongues]] — glossa (γλῶσσα), 1 Corinthians 14:2, 4-6, 9, 13-14, 18-19, 22-23, 26-27, 39
- interpretation — hermeneia (ἑρμηνεία) / diermeneuo (διερμηνεύω), 1 Corinthians 14:5, 13, 26-28
- edification/building up — oikodome (οἰκοδομή), 1 Corinthians 14:3-5, 12, 17, 26
- charisma (χάρισμα) — 1 Corinthians 12:4, 9, 28, 30-31

# Additional Information

- Paul frames the whole chapter around a single test for any pneumatikon: does it build up (oikodome) the assembly (1 Corinthians 14:3-5, 12, 26)?
- Tongues without interpretation edify only the speaker (1 Corinthians 14:4), while prophecy edifies the church.
- Paul explicitly ranks prophecy above tongues in corporate gathering for this reason (1 Corinthians 14:1, 5, 19).

# Connections

- 1 Corinthians 12:4-11 — the fuller list of charismata and their shared source, one Spirit (Pneuma)
- Romans 12:6-8 — charismata given "according to the grace given to us"
- Joel 2:28-29 — the promise that ruach would be poured out on all flesh
- Numbers 11:25 — the ruach on Moses distributed to the seventy elders, who then prophesied
- Acts 2:4, 17-18 — Pentecost as the fulfillment of Joel 2, tongues and prophecy appearing together
