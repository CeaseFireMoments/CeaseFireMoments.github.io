# Define

- Hebrew: tov (טוֹב) — functioning as intended; beautiful, pleasant, beneficial, whole, fitting; the root sense is _proper function and completeness_ — not merely moral virtue in the abstract but anything that is working as YHWH designed it; encompasses beauty, utility, pleasantness, and goodness simultaneously in a way that Greek thought tends to separate (Genesis 1:4, 10, 12, 18, 21, 25, 31; Deuteronomy 30:15)
- Greek (LXX): kalos (καλός) — beautiful, noble, excellent in form and character; the LXX translators primarily chose _kalos_ over _agathos_ (ἀγαθός) when rendering _tov_, particularly in creation texts, because _kalos_ carries both the aesthetic and moral dimension together, which better mirrors _tov_'s range; _agathos_ (ἀγαθός) is also used, leaning toward the functional/moral sense of good

# Related Words

- shalom (שָׁלוֹם) — wholeness, completeness, peace; functions as tov's relational and communal counterpart; Isaiah 45:7 sets shalom opposite ra, just as tov is set opposite ra (Deuteronomy 30:15)
- yashar (יָשָׁר) — upright, straight; often paired with tov to describe a life that is both morally right and functionally ordered (Deuteronomy 6:18; Micah 2:7)
- chesed (חֶסֶד) — covenant lovingkindness; closely linked to tov in the Psalms (Psalm 23:6; Psalm 100:5; Psalm 107:1)
- agathos (ἀγαθός) — NT Greek word for intrinsic goodness, moral excellence; used where tov's moral/beneficial dimension is primary

# Additional Information

- Every declaration of _ki tov_ (כִּי טוֹב) — "it was good" — in Genesis 1 is YHWH's assessment that what He made is _functioning as designed_, ordered, whole, and beautiful; this is not moral approval of an inanimate object but a declaration of proper alignment with His intent
- Genesis 1:31 — "God saw everything that He had made, and behold, it was very good (tov me'od)"; the intensifier _me'od_ (מְאֹד) marks the completion of all creation together as surpassing even each individual tov declaration
- Psalm 34:8 — "Oh taste and see that YHWH is tov" — tov used directly of YHWH's character; goodness is not just what He approves but what He _is_
- tov is also used of physical things: good land (Numbers 14:7), good trees (2 Kings 3:19), good fruit — always carrying the sense of that which is whole, healthy, and functioning rightly
