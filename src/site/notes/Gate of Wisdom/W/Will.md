# Define

- **Greek:** thelēma (θέλημα) — the will, desire, or purpose of one in authority; at time of writing carried the sense of active intention and determined purpose, not merely wish or preference. Rooted in thelō (θέλω) — to will, to desire with resolve. 
- **Hebrew equivalent:** ratson (רָצוֹן) — delight, favor, pleasure, will; what YHWH finds acceptable and takes pleasure in. Carries a relational warmth the Greek lacks — not merely command but what the Father delights in. 
	- Also chephets (חֵפֶץ) — desire, purpose, pleasure — used in Isaiah 53:10 "the chephets of YHWH will prosper in his hand."

# Related Words

- ratson (רָצוֹן) — favor, delight, acceptable will — Psalm 40:8, 143:10
- chephets (חֵפֶץ) — desire, purpose — Isaiah 53:10, 46:10
- boulē (βουλή) — counsel, determined plan — Acts 2:23, Ephesians 1:11
- thelō (θέλω) — to will, to [[desire\|desire]] — root of thelēma

# Additional Information

- 1 John 2:17 frames doing the will of God as the mark of the one who abides forever — set directly against the kosmos which is passing away
- Psalm 40:8 — "I delight to do your will my God — your Torah is within my heart" — the one who does YHWH's ratson is the one in whom Torah is internalized
- Yeshua uses thelēma consistently of the Father's authoritative purpose — John 4:34 "my food is to do the will of him who sent me," John 6:38 "I have come down from heaven not to do my own will but the will of him who sent me"
- Matthew 7:21 — "not everyone who says to me Lord Lord will enter the kingdom of heaven but he who does the will of my Father" — doing thelēma is the distinguishing mark, not confession alone
- The Hebrew mindset does not separate will from action — ratson is demonstrated through obedience, not merely affirmed through belief

# Connections

- Psalm 40:8 — delight in doing YHWH's will with Torah in the heart
- Isaiah 46:10 — YHWH declares his chephets will stand and he will accomplish all his purpose
- Isaiah 53:10 — the chephets of YHWH prospers through the servant
- John 6:38–40 — Yeshua defines his entire mission as doing the Father's thelēma
- Matthew 12:50 — whoever does the will of my Father is brother, sister, and mother
- Romans 12:2 — the renewed mind proves what the will of God is — good, acceptable, and perfect
- Hebrews 10:7 — quoting Psalm 40:8 and applying it directly to Yeshua's coming to do the Father's will
