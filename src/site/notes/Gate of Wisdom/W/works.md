- Hebrew: ma'aseh (מַעֲשֶׂה) — "work, deed, act, action"; from the root '-s-h ("to do, make"), ma'aseh denotes a concrete deed or action performed, not an inner disposition alone
- Greek (LXX): erga (ἔργα, plural of ergon ἔργον) — "works, deeds"

**Related words:**

- Asah (עָשָׂה) — the verb root itself, "to do, make," used earlier in the same verse for "turned from their evil way" is a different verb (shuv, שׁוּב, "to turn/return"), but ma'aseh directly derives from asah, the doing/making root
- Ma'alal (מַעֲלָל) — "deed, practice," a near-synonym often paired with or substituted for ma'aseh in prophetic contexts of moral evaluation
- Derekh (דֶּרֶךְ) — "way, path," used in the same verse ("turned from their evil way," דַּרְכָּם הָרָעָה) — pairing "way" (the course of life) with "works" (the concrete deeds along that course)

**Additional information:**

- Ma'aseh is a broad term used throughout the Tanakh both for human deeds (Genesis 44:15 — "what deed is this that you have done") and for divine works (Psalm 8:3 — "the work of your fingers")
- Ecclesiastes uses ma'aseh repeatedly to survey the totality of human labor and activity "under the sun" (Ecclesiastes 1:14; 2:11)

**Connections:**

- Jonah 3:5-8 — the sackcloth, fasting, and royal decree that precede and produce the ma'aseh YHWH evaluates in 3:10
- "God saw their works (ma'aseh), that they turned from their evil way. God relented of the disaster which he said he would do to them, and he didn't do it" (Jonah 3:10, WEB)
- James 2:18 — "show me your faith apart from your works, and I by my works will show you my faith" (WEB), a NT parallel emphasizing that deeds function as the visible evidence of an inward turning
- Ezekiel 33:11 — "I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked, but rather that the wicked turn from his way and live" (WEB) — the same turning-from-evil-way concept as Jonah 3:10, without the specific term ma'aseh
