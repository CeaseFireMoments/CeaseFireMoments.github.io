# Birth and Ancestors

- **Timeframe**: Returned to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes of Persia — Ezra 7:7-8
- **Place**: Born in Babylonian exile (exact city not stated)
- **Father**: Seraiah (as given in the priestly genealogy) — Ezra 7:1
- **Mother**: Not named
- **Sibling(s)**: Not named
- **Hebrew Meaning of Name**: Ezra (עֶזְרָא) — "help" or "helper"
- **Other Relatives of Note**: 
	- Traced in direct priestly line back to Aaron the chief priest — Ezra 7:1-5; 
	- descendant of Seraiah the high priest (also named in 2 Kings 25:18-21 and 1 Chronicles 6:14)

# Marriage and Descendants

- **Spouse #1**: Not mentioned in Scripture
    - **Children**: Not mentioned
- **Other Descendants of Note**: None named

# Land and Dwelling

## Dwelling Places

- Babylon (prior to return) — Ezra 7:6
- Jerusalem (after return) — Ezra 7:8; Nehemiah 8:1-2

## Journeys

- Led a group of exiles from Babylon to Jerusalem, a journey of four months — Ezra 7:6-9
- Departed from the river of Ahava — Ezra 8:21, 31

# Occupation and Military

- Priest — Ezra 7:11-12
- Scribe skilled in the Law of Moses — Ezra 7:6, 7:11
- No military role recorded

# Medical and Death

- Not recorded in Scripture

# Righteousness

- Described as one who set his heart to seek the Law of YHWH, to do it, and to teach statutes and judgments in Israel — Ezra 7:10
- The hand of YHWH his God was upon him — Ezra 7:6, 9, 28
- Led public confession and repentance over intermarriage with foreign nations — Ezra 9:3-6, 10:1
- Fasted and mourned over the unfaithfulness of the returned exiles — Ezra 9:3-5
- Read and explained the Law publicly, causing the people to understand — Nehemiah 8:1-8
- Led the people in a covenant renewal and observance of the Feast of Tabernacles — Nehemiah 8:13-18

# Unrighteousness

- None recorded

# Other

- Given a royal decree and resources from King Artaxerxes to beautify the house of YHWH and establish judges and teachers of the Law — Ezra 7:12-26
- Proclaimed a fast at the river Ahava to seek a safe journey rather than request a military escort — Ezra 8:21-23
- Confronted the sin of intermarriage with foreign peoples and led the dissolution of those marriages — Ezra 10:1-17
- Appears alongside Nehemiah at the public reading of the Law — Nehemiah 8:9, 12:26, 12:36

# Extra Biblical Information
