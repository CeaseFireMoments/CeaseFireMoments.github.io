# Birth and Ancestors

- **Timeframe**: Generations after the Flood, in the line of Ham (Genesis 10:1, 6-8)
- **Place**: Not explicitly stated; associated with the land of Shinar (Genesis 10:10)
- **Father**: Cush (Genesis 10:8; 1 Chronicles 1:10)
- **Mother**: Not named in Scripture
- **Sibling(s)**: Seba, Havilah, Sabtah, Raamah, and Sabteca, sons of Cush (Genesis 10:7); Nimrod is set apart from this list as a distinct, individually noted figure (Genesis 10:8)
- **Hebrew Meaning of Name**: Nimrod (נִמְרֹד) — commonly connected to the root marad (מָרַד), "to rebel"
- **Other Relatives of Note**: Grandson of Ham, great-grandson of Noah (Genesis 10:1, 6, 8)

# Marriage and Descendants

- **Spouse #1**: Not mentioned in Scripture
- **Other Descendants of Note**: None named

# Land and Dwelling

## Dwelling Places

- Not specified as a fixed dwelling; associated with the region of Shinar

## Journeys

- "Out of that land he went out into Assyria, and built [[Gate of Wisdom/Dwelling Places/Realm of Humans/Nineveh\|Nineveh]]" (Genesis 10:11, WEB) — a journey from Shinar to Assyria, building Nineveh, Rehoboth Ir, Calah, and Resen (Genesis 10:11-12)

# Occupation and Military

- "He began to be a mighty one in the earth" (Genesis 10:8, WEB)
- "He was a mighty hunter before YHWH" (Genesis 10:9, WEB), giving rise to the saying "like Nimrod, a mighty hunter before YHWH"
- Beginning of his kingdom included Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar (Genesis 10:10)
- Credited with the founding/building of Nineveh, Rehoboth Ir, Calah, and Resen, "the same is the great city" (Genesis 10:11-12)

# Medical and Death

- No death or burial account given in Scripture

# Righteousness

- None recorded in Scripture

# Unrighteousness

- None explicitly stated in the Genesis text itself; the description of him as "mighty" and builder of Babel's founding cities has been read by some in connection with Genesis 11:1-9 (the Tower of Babel), though the text does not directly name Nimrod in that account

# Other

- 1 Chronicles 1:10 repeats the Genesis notice: "Cush became the father of Nimrod; he began to be a mighty one in the earth" (WEB)
- Micah 5:6 refers to Assyria poetically as "the land of Nimrod" — "they will waste the land of Assyria with the sword, and the land of Nimrod in its gates" (WEB), linking his name permanently to Assyrian territory

# Extra Biblical Information

## Landmarks

- Traditionally associated with sites in ancient Mesopotamia, including the cities named in Genesis 10:10-12 (Babel/Babylon, Erech/Uruk, Accad/Akkad, Nineveh, Calah/Nimrud)

## Archaeology

- The site of Kalhu (biblical Calah, modern Nimrud, Iraq) bears a name traditionally linked to Nimrod, though this connection is extra-biblical and not established by the text itself
