## Key Scripture: John 20:21-22

Jesus, therefore, said to them again, \`Peace to you; according as the Father hath sent me, I also send you;’ and this having said, he breathed on *them*, and saith to them, \`Receive the Holy Spirit; (YLT)

### Observations

#### a. Keywords

- Breathed (g1720)- emphusaō -to puff; to blow at or on: – breathe on.
- Receive (g2983) – lambanō – to take (in very many applications, literally and figuratively \[probably objective or active, to get hold of;
- Holy Spirit (4151)- pneuma – the third person of the triune God, the Holy Spirit, coequal, coeternal with the Father and the Son

#### c. Notes on Leading Scripture:

- Jesus is speaking.
- Jesus said, as the father sent me, I also send you
- Breathed on them
- Receive the Holy Spirit

#### d. Notes In Relation To Other Scriptures

- No where else uses this same word breath in regards to receiving the Holy Spirit.
- Receive by faith, believing, beholding, and knowing Jesus.
- Receive power and become witnesses
- Peter and John laid hands on and then they received.
- Received to know things of God, spiritual things.
- Supplies mighty acts
- Evidences
	- No evidence recorded in Acts 8:15-17
		- Tongues {human languages} and magnifying God
