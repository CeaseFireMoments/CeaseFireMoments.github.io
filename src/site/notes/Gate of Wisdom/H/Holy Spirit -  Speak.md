## Holy Spirit – Speak

## Key Scripture: Matthew 10:18-20

### 18And you will be brought before governors and kings for My sake, as a witness to bear testimony before them and to the Gentiles (the nations). 19But when they deliver you up, do not be anxious about how or what you are to speak; for what you are to say will be given you in that very hour and \[f\]moment, 20For it is not you who are speaking, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you. (AMP)

### Observations

#### a. Keywords

- Holy Spirit (4151)- pneuma – the third person of the triune God, the Holy Spirit, coequal, coeternal with the Father and the Son
- Speak (G2980) – laleō – A prolonged form of an otherwise obsolete verb; to talk, that is, utter words: – preach, say, speak (after), talk, tell, utter.
