# Define

- Greek: sōzō (σῴζω) — to save, deliver, rescue, preserve from harm or destruction; used of both physical deliverance and ultimate deliverance from judgment
- Hebrew: yasha (יָשַׁע) — to save, deliver, bring into a wide/open place out of distress; root of the name Yeshua

# Related Words

- yeshuah (יְשׁוּעָה) — salvation, deliverance — Psalm 3:8, Isaiah 12:2
- sōtēria (σωτηρία) — salvation — Romans 1:16

# Additional Information

- Mark 16:16 pairs belief/[[Gate of Wisdom/B/believes\|believes]] and baptism as the path to being saved, with unbelief as the sole cause named for condemnation

# Connections

- Isaiah 12:2, Psalm 3:8, Acts 4:12, Romans 10:9-10, John 3:17
