# Define

- Greek: euangelion (εὐαγγέλιον) — "good news, good tidings." 
	- Scripture itself defines its content rather than external usage: 
		- Mark 1:1 it is the euangelion of Yeshua the Messiah
		- Romans 1:1-4 Paul defines it as the message concerning God's Son, descended from David, declared Son of God by the resurrection
		- 1 Corinthians 15:1-4 he defines it explicitly as the death, burial, and resurrection of Messiah according to the Scriptures.
- Hebrew: besorah (בְּשׂוֹרָה) — from basar (בָּשַׂר), "to bring/announce news." 
	- Isaiah 52:7 and Isaiah 61:1 define the content of the besorah as the reign of YHWH, salvation, and release — the same content Yeshua applies to himself in Luke 4:18-19.
# Related Words

- euangelizo (εὐαγγελίζω) — verb form, "to proclaim good news, to evangelize"
- basar (בָּשַׂר) — Hebrew verb, "to bring/announce good news," used in 2 Samuel 18:19-31, Isaiah 40:9, Isaiah 52:7, Isaiah 61:1
- malak (מַלְאָךְ) — "[[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Messenger\|Messenger]]," the one who carries the besorah 

# Additional Information

- Isaiah 61:1 — "The Spirit of the Lord YHWH is on me; because YHWH has anointed me to preach good news to the humble" — Yeshua applies this verse to himself in Luke 4:18
- Isaiah 52:7 — "How beautiful on the mountains are the feet of him who brings good news" — quoted directly in Romans 10:15 regarding those who proclaim the gospel
- Mark 1:1 opens his account with euangelion, framing the entire narrative as the fulfillment of the prophetic herald motif from Isaiah  
- Nahum 1:15 uses the same herald imagery for the announcement of judgment on Nineveh and peace for Judah
- Yeshua's own summary of his message is given as "the gospel of the Kingdom" (Matthew 4:23, Mark 1:14-15) — repentance is the required response to the announcement
- Paul defines the content of the gospel explicitly in 1 Corinthians 15:1-4 as the death, burial, and resurrection of Messiah according to the Scriptures

# Connections

- The besorah of Isaiah is the OT root of the NT euangelion — both describe a herald's announcement that YHWH is acting to save and reign
- Romans 1:16 — Paul calls the gospel "the power of God for salvation," tying it to the same saving action the Isaiah herald announces
- The gospel is consistently framed in Scripture as news of an event and a reign, not merely a doctrine — an announcement demanding a response of repentance and allegiance (Mark 1:15)

# Gospel Perspectives by People

**Yeshua** — [Mark 1:14-15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%201:14-15&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "The time is fulfilled, and God's Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News." Defines it as the nearness of God's Kingdom, requiring repentance and belief.

* [Luke 4:18-19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%204:18-19&version=WEB;NET;OJB): applying Isaiah 61 to himself — good news to the poor, release to captives, recovery of sight to the blind, freedom for the oppressed, the acceptable year of YHWH
* [Matthew 4:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%204:23&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "the Good News of the Kingdom" — paired throughout his ministry with healing

**Peter** — [Acts 10:36-43](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%2010:36-43&version=WEB;NET;OJB): the message God sent to Israel through Yeshua the Messiah; his anointing by the Spirit; his going about doing good and healing; his death on a tree; his resurrection on the third day and appearances to chosen witnesses; that he is ordained judge of the living and dead; that everyone believing in him receives remission of sins through his name

* [Acts 2:22-38](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%202:22-38&version=WEB;NET;OJB): Yeshua of Nazareth, attested by miracles, delivered up and killed, raised by God, exalted to God's right hand, made both Lord and Messiah — response required is repentance and baptism

**Philip** — [Acts 8:35](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%208:35&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "beginning from this Scripture" (Isaiah 53), preached Yeshua to the eunuch — the gospel content tied directly to the suffering servant

* [Acts 8:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%208:12&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "good news concerning God's Kingdom and the name of Yeshua Messiah"

**Paul** — [1 Corinthians 15:1-4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Corinthians%2015:1-4&version=WEB;NET;OJB): the gospel he received and preached — Messiah died for our sins according to the Scriptures, was buried, was raised the third day according to the Scriptures, and appeared

* [Romans 1:1-4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%201:1-4&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "the Good News of God... concerning his Son, who was born of David's offspring... who was declared to be the Son of God with power... by the resurrection from the dead"
* [Romans 1:16-17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%201:16-17&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "the power of God for salvation for everyone who believes... For in it is revealed God's righteousness from faith to faith"
* [Galatians 1:11-12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians%201:11-12&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "the Good News which was preached by me is not according to man... I received it through revelation of Yeshua Messiah"
* [Acts 20:24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%2020:24&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "the Good News of the grace of God"
* [Acts 13:32-33](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%2013:32-33&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "the good news of the promise made to the fathers... God has fulfilled the same to us... in that he raised up Yeshua"

**John** (angel's proclamation, recorded by John) — [Revelation 14:6-7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation%2014:6-7&version=WEB;NET;OJB): "an eternal Good News to proclaim... Fear God, and give him glory... worship him who made the heaven, the earth, the sea, and the springs of waters" — gospel here defined as a call to fear and worship the Creator in view of judgment

**Isaiah** (besorah, foundational OT definition later applied by Yeshua and Paul) — [Isaiah 52:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah%2052:7&version=WEB;NET;OJB): good news of peace, good tidings of good, salvation, and "Your God reigns!"
