# Define

- **Hebrew**: arel (עָרֵל) — uncircumcised; one who has the foreskin (orlah/עָרְלָה) still intact. The root carries the sense of being covered, blocked, or closed off. In the Hebrew mindset the term extends well beyond the physical — it describes anything that is sealed off, unfit, or not yet consecrated for use or covenant participation.
- **Greek (LXX)**: akrobustia (ἀκροβυστία) — foreskin, uncircumcision; the physical state of being uncircumcised. The Greek term is more narrowly physical than the Hebrew, which carries the broader sense of being blocked or unready.

# Related Words

- orlah (עָרְלָה) — foreskin; the noun form; used both of the physical foreskin and of the fruit of new trees in Leviticus 19:23
- mul (מוּל) — to circumcise; the verb of cutting away the orlah
- arel sefatayim (עֲרַל שְׂפָתַיִם) — uncircumcised of lips; Moses describes himself this way in Exodus 6:12, 30 — blocked, unfit, unable to speak effectively
- arel lev (עֲרַל לֵב) — uncircumcised of heart; used in Leviticus 26:41; Deuteronomy 10:16; Jeremiah 4:4 — a heart that is closed off, unresponsive to God

# Additional Information

- The application of arel to the fruit of new trees in Leviticus 19:23 is the clearest evidence that the term operates beyond the physical — the fruit is not a person and cannot be literally circumcised; it is sealed off and unfit for use during the first three years
- Exodus 6:12 — Moses calls himself arel sefatayim — _"I am of uncircumcised lips"_ (WEB) — blocked, unready, unfit for the task
- Deuteronomy 10:16 — _"Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart"_ (WEB) — the command to remove what blocks the heart from full covenant responsiveness
- Jeremiah 4:4 — _"Circumcise yourselves to Yahweh, and take away the foreskins of your heart"_ (WEB)
- Leviticus 26:41 — God describes Israel in exile as having an uncircumcised heart — blocked from receiving what He offers
- Ezekiel 44:7-9 — uncircumcised of heart and flesh used together as the disqualifying condition for entering the sanctuary

# Connections

- Leviticus 19:23 — source passage; orlah applied to fruit
- Leviticus 26:41 — uncircumcised heart as covenant blockage
- Deuteronomy 10:16; 30:6 — command and promise of heart circumcision
- Jeremiah 4:4 — prophetic call to heart circumcision
- Ezekiel 44:7-9 — uncircumcised heart and flesh as disqualifying conditions
- Romans 2:28-29 — _"he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit not in the letter"_ (WEB) — Paul drawing on the full Hebrew range of arel and orlah
- Colossians 2:11 — _"in whom you were also circumcised with a circumcision not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the sins of the flesh, in the circumcision of Messiah"_ (WEB)
- Acts 7:51 — Stephen calls the council _"stiff-necked and uncircumcised in heart and ears"_ (WEB) — drawing directly on the Hebrew prophetic usage
