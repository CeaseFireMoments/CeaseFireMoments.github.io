{ .block-language-dataview}## Holy Spirit Filling

## Key Scripture: Acts 2:1-4

And in the day of the Pentecost being fulfilled, they were all with one accord at the same place, and there came suddenly out of the heaven a sound as of a bearing violent breath, and it filled all the house where they were sitting, and there appeared to them divided tongues, as it were of fire; it sat also upon each one of them, and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit, and began to speak with other tongues, according as the Spirit was giving them to declare. (YLT)

- Verse 4 AMP: And they were all filled (diffused throughout their souls) with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other (different, foreign) languages (tongues), as the Spirit \[a\]kept giving them clear and loud expression \[in each tongue in appropriate words\].

### Observations

#### a. Keywords

- Filled (g4130) – plēthō – to “fill” (literally or figuratively \[imbue, influence, supply\]); specifically to fulfil (time): – accomplish, full (... come), furnish.
- Holy Spirit (g4151) – pneuma – the third person of the triune God, the Holy Spirit, coequal, coeternal with the Father and the Son
- Speak (g2980) – laleō – A prolonged form of an otherwise obsolete verb; to talk, that is, utter words: – preach, say, speak (after), talk, tell, utter.
- Tongues (g1100) – a tongue; the language or dialect used by a particular people distinct from that of other nations
- Declare (g669) – apophtheggomai – to enunciate plainly, that is, declare: – say, speak forth, utterance
