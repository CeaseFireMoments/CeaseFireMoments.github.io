# Define

- **Greek:** kosmos (κόσμος) — the ordered system; at time of writing referred to the structured arrangement of human society, values, authorities, and power operating apart from God. Not merely physical creation but the whole present age and its governing values. Greek mindset carried the sense of beauty and order in the arrangement — but John subverts this by framing it as the domain under the archōn (John 12:31).
- **Hebrew equivalent:** olam (עוֹלָם) — age, duration, the present world-age as distinct from the age to come. Also tevel (תֵּבֵל) for the inhabited world as physical domain (Psalm 24:1), and eretz (אֶרֶץ) for earth/land in the more concrete sense. olam is the most theologically loaded — "this olam" versus "the olam to come" is a foundational Hebrew distinction that underlies John's warning.

# Related Words

- aiōn (αἰών) — age, used in "this age" versus "the age to come"; closely parallel to olam; Ephesians 2:2 uses it alongside kosmos
- eretz (אֶרֶץ) — [[Zunpublished/Earth\|Earth]], land, Genesis 1:1
- tevel (תֵּבֵל) — inhabited world, Psalm 24:1, 98:7
- archōn tou kosmou (ἄρχων τοῦ κόσμου) — ruler of this world, John 12:31, 14:30, 16:11

# Additional Information

- 1 John 2:16 defines what "the world" consists of — the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life — making clear kosmos here is a value system not a physical location
- 1 John 2:17 declares the kosmos is passing away — Greek: paragetai (παράγεται), presently in the process of passing, not future — placing it firmly in the olam framework of a present age already under judgment
- John 12:31 establishes the kosmos has a ruler who has been judged and cast out — the kosmos John warns against is therefore not neutral creation but a domain under enemy administration
- Genesis 1 uses eretz and the creation account to establish the physical world as YHWH's — the kosmos of John is what has been built on top of that by human rebellion and enemy influence

# Connections

- John 12:31 — now the ruler of this kosmos will be driven out
- John 17:14–16 — Yeshua's prayer that his followers are not of the kosmos even as he is not of the kosmos
- James 4:4 — friendship with the world is enmity with God
- Isaiah 13:11 — YHWH declares judgment on the olam for its evil
- 2 Thessalonians 2:4 — the man of lawlessness operating within and through the world-system
- Revelation 11:15 — the kingdom of the kosmos becomes the kingdom of YHWH and his Messiah
