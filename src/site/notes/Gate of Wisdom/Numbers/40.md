Hebrew word: Arba'im (אַרְבָּעִים)

- Meaning: Forty
- Represents: Testing, trial, probation, transition — a recurring period preceding judgment, purification, or a new phase
- Paleo-Hebrew symbol: The letter mem (מ) in Paleo-Hebrew script is pictographic of water/waves — Note: this letter-symbolism is a matter of Paleo-Hebrew script tradition, not something Scripture itself states
- Hebrew character representing the number: מ (mem), used as the numeral value 40 in the Hebrew alphanumeric system
- Significant verses in the Bible:
    - Genesis 7:4, 12: "I will cause it to rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights" (WEB) — the Flood
    - Exodus 24:18: "Moses entered into the midst of the cloud... Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights" (WEB)
    - Exodus 34:28: Moses again "was there with Yahweh forty days and forty nights" at the giving of the second tablets (WEB)
    - Numbers 13:25; 14:33-34: the spies scout the land forty days, resulting in forty years of wilderness wandering — "after the number of the days in which you spied out the land, even forty days, for every day a year, you will bear your iniquities" (WEB)
    - 1 Samuel 17:16: Goliath presents himself for forty days before David's confrontation (WEB)
    - 1 Kings 19:8: Elijah travels "forty days and forty nights to Horeb, the Mount of God" (WEB)
    - Jonah 3:4: "Yet forty days, and Nineveh will be overthrown" (WEB)
    - Matthew 4:2 / Luke 4:2: Yeshua "fasted forty days and forty nights" in the wilderness before being tempted (WEB)
    - Acts 1:3: Yeshua appeared to the apostles "by the space of forty days" after His resurrection, before ascending (WEB)
