# Define

- Hebrew: ra (רַע) — broken, dysfunctional, harmful, displeasing; from the root ra'a' (רָעַע), meaning to break or to ruin; in Hebrew thought ra is not primarily a cosmic moral force but anything that is out of alignment with YHWH's intended design — whether physical defect, calamity, moral wickedness, or that which causes distress and harm (Genesis 6:5; Genesis 41:19; Deuteronomy 30:15; Isaiah 45:7)
- Greek (LXX): two words are used depending on context —
    - kakos (κακός) — bad in quality or character, the broader term; used in Isaiah 45:7
    - poneros (πονηρός) — active, injurious, harmful in effect; the stronger word, used in Genesis 2:9, 17 for the tree of knowledge of good and evil

# Related Words

- ra'a' (רָעַע) — the root verb; to be evil, to spoil, to break in pieces (Jeremiah 11:16; Job 34:24)
- ra'ah (רָעָה) — calamity, misfortune; the feminine form used for disaster or harm (Amos 6:3; Proverbs 22:3)
- aven (אָוֶן) — worthlessness, vanity, mischief; often paired with ra in the phrase "workers of iniquity" (Psalm 5:5; Job 4:8)
- avon (עָוֹן) — iniquity, guilt carried as a consequence; frequently translated alongside ra (Isaiah 53:6)

# Additional Information

- Isaiah 45:7 is one of the most striking ra texts: "I form the light and create darkness. I make peace and create calamity (ra). I am YHWH, who does all these things." — ra here is set directly opposite shalom (שָׁלוֹם), wholeness; this pairing reveals that the Hebrew mind understood ra as _brokenness_ — the absence of shalom, not merely the presence of wickedness
- The tree of the knowledge of tov and ra (Genesis 2:9, 17) uses ra in its broadest possible sense — not merely moral evil but the full experience of dysfunction, harm, and disorder apart from YHWH's ordering
- ra is used of physical things: sickly cows (Genesis 41:19-20), a poisonous pot of stew (2 Kings 4:41), malignant boils (Deuteronomy 28:35) — showing the word describes functional brokenness as much as moral evil

# Connections

- Genesis 2:9, 17 — first appearance; the tree of the knowledge of tov and ra
- Genesis 6:5 — every inclination of the thoughts of man's heart was only ra continually
- Deuteronomy 30:15 — "I have set before you today life and tov, and death and ra"
- Isaiah 5:20 — "Woe to those who call ra tov and tov ra"

- Proverbs 8:13 — "The fear of YHWH is to hate ra"
- Amos 5:14-15 — "Seek tov and not ra, that you may live"
