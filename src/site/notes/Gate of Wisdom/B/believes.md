# Define

- Greek: pisteuō (πιστεύω) — to believe, to have faith in, to entrust oneself to; at the time of writing carried the sense of active reliance and allegiance, not mere mental assent
- Hebrew: aman (אָמַן) — to be firm, to support, to trust; root of "amen" — a Hebrew mindset of standing firmly upon something reliable, rather than simply agreeing with a proposition

# Related Words

- pistis (πίστις) — faith, faithfulness — John 3:16, Romans 10:9-10
- aman (אָמַן) — Genesis 15:6, Habakkuk 2:4

# Additional Information

- The pairing of believing and baptism in this verse ties inward trust to an outward act — Acts 2:38, Romans 6:3-4

# Connections

- John 3:16, John 3:36, Romans 10:9-10, Habakkuk 2:4
