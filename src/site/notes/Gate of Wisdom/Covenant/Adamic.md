# Define
- Made with Adam
- Humanity's dominion over creation and life in fellowship with YHWH
- Genesis 1-3

# What was given
- Dominion over the earth (Genesis 1:26–28)
- Every seed-bearing plant and fruit for food (Genesis 1:29)
- Fellowship with their Creator
- A home in the Garden of Eden
- The opportunity for eternal life through access to the Tree of Life (Genesis 2:9).

# Terms
- “Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat: But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it…” Genesis 2:16–17

#  Status
- Broken by Adam
