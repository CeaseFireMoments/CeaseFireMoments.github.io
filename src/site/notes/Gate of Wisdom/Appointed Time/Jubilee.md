Word: Jubilee — Leviticus 25:10

# Define

- **Hebrew**: yovel (יוֹבֵל) — ram, ram's horn, trumpet blast. The word refers first to the instrument — the shofar made from a ram's horn — and by extension to the year proclaimed by its blast. The Jubilee is named for the sound that announces it, not for the concept of freedom itself. The ram's horn blast on Yom Kippur in the fiftieth year sets everything in motion.
- **Greek (LXX)**: aphesis (ἄφεσις) — release, dismissal, forgiveness. The LXX translators chose a word that captures the _effect_ of the Jubilee rather than its Hebrew name — release of debts, release of persons, release of land. Aphesis is the same word used in the NT for forgiveness of sins.

# Related Words

- deror (דְּרוֹר) — liberty, release; the word used in Leviticus 25:10 for what is proclaimed — _"proclaim liberty throughout the land"_; also used in Isaiah 61:1 and Jeremiah 34:8
- shemitah (שְׁמִטָּה) — the seventh-year release of debts and the land's rest; the Jubilee operates on the same principle but at the fiftieth year
- yom kippur (יוֹם כִּפּוּר) —[[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/Day of Atonement\|Day of Atonement]]; the Jubilee is inaugurated on Yom Kippur (Leviticus 25:9), making atonement the foundation from which liberty is proclaimed
- aphesis (ἄφεσις) — NT Greek for forgiveness/release; Luke 4:18 uses it directly in Yeshua's reading of Isaiah 61

# Additional Information

- The Jubilee is the fiftieth year — seven cycles of seven years plus one (Leviticus 25:8-10)
- It is announced by the blowing of the shofar on the tenth day of the seventh month — Yom Kippur (Leviticus 25:9)
- Three things are released in the Jubilee year: persons return to their families, land returns to its original owners, and debts are structured around it (Leviticus 25:10-28)
- The land itself is not to be sold permanently — _"the land shall not be sold forever, for the land is mine"_ (Leviticus 25:23, WEB) — the Jubilee enforces this by returning all land to its covenant allotment
- Isaiah 61:1-2 — _"to proclaim liberty (deror) to the captives... to proclaim the year of Yahweh's favor"_ — the prophetic extension of Jubilee language into eschatological promise
- Luke 4:18-19 — Yeshua reads Isaiah 61 in the synagogue at Nazareth and declares _"Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing"_ (Luke 4:21, WEB) — placing himself as the one who inaugurates the ultimate Jubilee
- The use of aphesis in both the LXX Jubilee passages and the NT forgiveness passages is not incidental — release of debt, release of persons, and forgiveness of sins share the same word and the same conceptual root

# Connections

- Leviticus 25:8-55 — full Jubilee legislation
- Leviticus 25:9 — Yom Kippur as the inauguration of Jubilee
- Leviticus 25:23 — the land belongs to God; Israel are sojourners and tenants
- Deuteronomy 15:1-18 — shemitah legislation; the seventh-year pattern underlying Jubilee
- Isaiah 61:1-2 — prophetic Jubilee language
- Jeremiah 34:8-17 — failure to proclaim release judged directly by God
- Luke 4:16-21 — Yeshua's declaration of Jubilee fulfillment
- Luke 4:18 — aphesis used for both release of captives and forgiveness — the Jubilee and atonement unified in Yeshua
