# Define

- Hebrew: Yom Teruah (יוֹם תְּרוּעָה) — "day of shouting/blasting"; 
	- a memorial of blowing of trumpets, 
	- a [[Gate of Wisdom/H/Holy\|Holy]] convocation on the first day[^1] of the seventh month[^2] (Tishrei), 
	- a [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/Sabbath\|Sabbath]] in which 
		- no servile work is done, 
		- an offering made by fire to YHWH (Leviticus 23:24-25). 
	- Numbers 29:1 calls it "a day of blowing the trumpets" (yom teruah), marked out from the other [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/New Moon\|New Moon]]s. 
	- The root ruah/teruah (רוּעַ) conveys a loud shout, alarm, or blast — used both for battle alarm (Numbers 10:9) and for joyful acclamation (Psalm 47:5).
- Greek (LXX): the Septuagint renders this mnēmosynon salpingōn (μνημόσυνον σαλπίγγων), "a memorial of trumpets," 
	- in Leviticus 23:24. Salpinx (σάλπιγξ) is the standard Greek word for trumpet, carried into the NT — used of the trumpet call at the resurrection (1 Corinthians 15:52) and the trumpets of Revelation (Revelation 8:2).

# Related Words

- shofar (שׁוֹפָר) — ram's horn trumpet; distinct from the silver trumpets (chatzotzerah, חֲצֹצְרָה) of Numbers 10:1-10, though both may be in view in the "blowing" tradition
- zikkaron (זִכָּרוֹן) — "memorial, remembrance"; Leviticus 23:24 calls the day zikkaron teruah, a "memorial of blowing"
- mikra qodesh (מִקְרָא קֹדֶשׁ) — "[[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/Holy Convocation\|Holy Convocation]]," (Leviticus 23:24)
- [[Gate of Wisdom/Appointed Time/SabbathS\|SabbathS]]

# Additional Information

- Falls on the first day of the seventh month, the same new moon that begins the civil/agricultural year cycle culminating in Yom Kippur (tenth day) and Sukkot (fifteenth day) of that same month (Leviticus 23:23-43)
- Commanded as a statute of rest and convocation — [[Gate of Wisdom/Right Standing/Statutes (Chuqim)\|Statutes (Chuqim)]]
- Numbers 29:2-6 specifies the additional [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Burnt Offering\|Burnt Offering]]s for the day: one young bull, one ram, seven lambs, with their [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Grain Offering\|Grain Offering]] and [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Drink Offering\|Drink Offering]]s, plus a [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Sin Offering\|Sin Offering]], in addition to the regular monthly and daily [[Gate of Wisdom/Offering/Offerings\|Offerings]]

# Connections

- Numbers 10:9-10 links trumpet blowing both to war (a remembrance before YHWH against enemies) and to appointed feasts and new moons, over burnt offerings and peace offerings, "for a memorial before your God"
- Psalm 81:3-4 — "Blow the trumpet at the New Moon, at the full moon, on our feast day. For it is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of [[Gate of Wisdom/Beings/Human Beings/Jacob\|Jacob]]" — commonly associated with this feast
- Joel 2:1, 2:15 — trumpet blown in [[Gate of Wisdom/Z/Zion\|Zion]] as an alarm calling for a solemn assembly and repentance, thematically tied to the shofar-blast day
- 1 Corinthians 15:52 and 1 Thessalonians 4:16 reference a trumpet sounding in connection with resurrection and gathering — inferred connection to Yom Teruah's trumpet theme, not stated directly in the text
- Revelation 8:2, 8:6 — seven trumpets sounded in judgment sequence — inferred thematic connection, not a direct textual link
